About

Hello! I'm Cedrick, a BS Information Technology student from Cebu, Philippines. I'm passionate about building technology that brings ideas to life. My journey started with a curiosity for how computers and networks operate, which naturally evolved into a love for web development and interface design.

Outside of school, I work as a freelance web developer and digital designer. I partner with individuals and local businesses to create tailored websites, branding materials, apparel designs, and promotional graphics. This hands-on experience has sharpened my technical abilities and taught me how to communicate effectively with clients to meet their real-world needs.

To me, great technology goes beyond just writing code it's about building solutions that are reliable, accessible, and practical. Whether I'm fixing a hardware issue, setting up a local network, coding a web app, or designing a brand identity, my focus is always on delivering work that truly helps people.

I'm currently pursuing my degree while continuing to expand my skill set in Networking, System Administration, Cloud Technologies, and Full-Stack Web Development.
